# Pocket Band: a demo you can run through

About eight minutes, for Pocket Band 0.4. Each step says what to press, what should happen, and a line
you can say. The full reference is [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md).

Sketches are different every time, so the notes you hear will not match anyone else's. The steps do
not depend on a particular sketch.

**Before you start**

- Power on and wait for the Stage. Turn MASTER to a comfortable level.
- If the FM-1 remembers an earlier sketch, that is fine; step 1 replaces it.
- Knob 1 to 4 are the four knobs named along the bottom of the screen.

---

## 1. One press to a track (Map)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **GLO** | The world map. The band's marker stands in a town. |
| **KNOB 3** right until the bottom reads **HOUSE** | The marker jumps from town to town. |
| **GLO** | "HOUSE" in the top bar, the Stage opens, music plays. |

*Say:* "That is a whole four-part track, written just now for that town. Drums, bass, keys, lead, one
key, one tempo."

## 2. Meet the band (Stage)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **ALGORITHM** right, slowly, four clicks | The frame moves across drums, bass, keys, lead and back. |
| Stop on **BASS**. **KNOB 1** right one click | IDEA reads 2 at the bottom: the bass line changes, the rest carries on. |
| **KNOB 1** left one click | The first bass line comes back, note for note. |
| **KNOB 2** left about six clicks, then right again | Parts thin out, then fill back in. |
| Select **LEAD**. **HOME** | "LEAD SLEEPS" or "LEAD WAKES UP"; the sprite greys out or lights up. |

*Say:* "Every member can be re-rolled on its own, and nothing is lost: turning back gives you the part
you had. Energy thins the whole band without changing what it plays."

## 3. Blend two genres (Map)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **GLO** | The map. |
| **KNOB 1** and **KNOB 2** to walk between two towns | Two lines to the nearest towns; the bar shows the mix. |
| **GLO** | A blended sketch, for example "HOUSE MIX". |

*Say:* "Between towns you get a blend: the tempo sits between the two, and each member comes from one
town or the other."

## 4. Audition a new player (Audition)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| Select **BASS** with ALGORITHM, then **EDIT** | Four cards: NOW and three other bass players, each with a FIT score. |
| **KNOB 1** right, one click at a time | Each player takes over the bass in the mix as you land on it. |
| Stop on one you like. **EDIT** | "HIRED". |

*Say:* "These are recommendations, not presets. Each is scored on how it sits with what the rest of the
band is playing, and you hear it in context before you commit."

## 5. Change the sound (Sound)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **ENV** | The member's sound, with four bars. |
| **PRESETS** right a few clicks | The sound's name changes; the part keeps playing with the new sound. |
| **KNOB 1** and **KNOB 2** | The sound's own main controls move. |
| **ENV** | Page SHAPE. **KNOB 3** left closes the filter; return it to the middle. |
| **ENV** | Page SPACE. **KNOB 1** adds reverb. |

*Say:* "A hundred and fifty sounds, and for each one just four knobs a page instead of a synth panel."

## 6. Change key and chords (Key)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **SEL** | The key in large letters, a keyboard with the scale lit, four chord boxes. |
| **KNOB 1** right two clicks | The key changes and bass, keys and lead are rewritten in it, still in tune with each other. |
| **SEL** | "NEW CHORDS": a different progression, the parts follow. |
| Play a few **keys** | Every key lands on a note of the scale. |

*Say:* "I cannot play a wrong note. The keys only give me notes from the key the band is in."

## 7. Play it like a DJ (Perform)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **FX** | A grid of effect names. |
| Hold the **lowest key** | "L4" lights and a short piece of the music loops. Let go and it carries on in time. |
| Hold a few other keys, one at a time | Stutter, reverse, tape stop, half speed and so on. |
| Hold **OCT+** for a beat | An echo throw. |
| **KNOB 1** left, then back to the middle | The whole mix darkens, then opens. |
| **FX** | "BUILD: FROM THE NEXT BAR", then two bars of snare roll and rising filter, then "DROP!". |

*Say:* "All 27 keys are effects here, held for as long as you want them. And one press sets up a
build and a drop on the bar."

## 8. Let the band jam (Jam)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **LFO** | The band, "THE BAND WAITS". |
| **LFO** | One member's part changes; the screen names it. |
| **OCT−** | "KILLED": the part goes back. |
| **LFO**, then **OCT+** | "KEPT". |
| **KNOB 1** right to **4** | Now a part changes by itself every four bars. |
| **KNOB 3** right a few clicks | MORPH: every part leans toward its next idea. |
| **KNOB 1** left to **OFF**, **KNOB 3** back to **0** | The jam stops; the parts are as they were. |

*Say:* "The band can evolve on its own, and I only decide what stays."

## 9. Tap a rhythm in (REC)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **HOME**, select **LEAD** | The Stage. |
| **REC** | A red square in the top bar: recording. |
| **REC** again | A gold square, "TAP: ANY KEY, PART EMPTIED". The lead goes quiet. |
| Tap any keys in a rhythm for a bar or two | Each tap sounds as a note that fits the chord, and it is there on the next pass. |
| **REC** | "REC OFF". |

*Say:* "I only supplied the rhythm. The notes were chosen for me from the chords."

## 10. Edit by hand (Grid)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **SEQ** | The lead's part as a grid: steps across, notes up. |
| **KNOB 1** to move along, **KNOB 2** to move up and down | A cursor moves. |
| **SEQ** | A note appears at the cursor. **SEQ** again removes it. |
| **ALGORITHM** to **DRUMS** | The grid shows drum sounds instead. |

*Say:* "And when I want exact control, every note of every part is here."

## 11. The game (Visuals)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **ARP** | A scope of the sound. |
| **ARP** three times | Meters, stars, then "RUN!". |
| **ARP** | The game starts. Blocks and beams come in from the right, in time with the drums. |
| **High keys** (or OCT+) to jump blocks, **low keys** (or OCT−) to duck beams | Score and combo climb. |
| Miss on purpose | A heart goes, a band member falls asleep, the music thins. |

*Say:* "The level is the track. Kicks are jumps, snares are ducks, and every sketch is a new level."

Leave with **HOME**.

## 12. Keep it (Cart)

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **SAVE** | Page CODE: the sketch's six-letter code. |
| **KNOB 1** right | Page SLOTS. |
| **SAVE** | "STORED" in slot A. |
| **KNOB 1** right, **KNOB 2** to **SKIN**, **KNOB 3** | Page SETUP; the colours change. |

*Say:* "A six-letter code rebuilds this exact sketch on any FM-1 running Pocket Band. The slots keep
my edits."

## 13. Finish

| Press | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| **PLAY** | The music stops. |
| Wait about twenty seconds, switch off, switch on | The same sketch is back; PLAY carries on with it. |

---

## Extras if there is time

- **Tour:** LFO, KNOB 4 to 16. Every 16 bars the band moves on along the map by itself.
- **Chain:** store a second sketch in slot B, then on SLOTS set KNOB 3 to CHAIN and press SAVE while
  playing. The slots play in turn.
- **A code to type in:** on Cart's CODE page, set the six letters to `AACAAA` and press SAVE for a
  House sketch at 126. KNOB 2 picks the letter, KNOB 3 changes it.
- **Hidden:** walk off the south-east corner of the map until a "?" blinks, and travel. Or enter the
  code `PARTY2`.

## If something does not match

- **"NO SKETCH: GLO, THEN GLO":** nothing has been generated yet. Do step 1.
- **A knob seems to do nothing:** check the names along the bottom; knobs change with the screen.
- **No sound from a member:** it is asleep. Select it on the Stage and press HOME.
- **"PRESS PLAY FIRST":** the build and the chain need the music running.
- **The keys make no notes:** you are on Perform (they are effects), in the run, or in tap mode.
