POCKET BAND

Pocket Band

A playable prototype of the new FM-1 firmware. Press PLAY on the device to start: it builds a full track, and the eight band members on stage are its eight tracks.

FIRST MINUTE

Press GLO to open the world map. Turn the first two knobs to walk between towns, then press GLO again to travel there. Standing between two towns blends their styles. Press FX and tap the keys to launch sections, fills and drops.

Knobs: drag up or down, scroll over them, or tap the left or right half. Each button opens a screen; pressing the same button again does that screen's main action.

BUTTONS

HOME
Stage. Pick a member with knob 1. Press again to put them to sleep or wake them. Turn knob 7 right for a new idea for that part, left to go back to earlier ones.
GLO
World map. Press again to travel and get a new sketch.
EDIT
Audition. Three replacement parts ranked by how well they fit. Turn PICK to hear each in the mix, press again to hire.
ENV
Sound. Shape the selected member's sound with four plain knobs. Press again to roll the dice.
LFO
Evolve and morph. Blend every part toward its B version, or let parts mutate and judge each change with OCT- and OCT+.
SEQ
Grid. Edit any part step by step. Press again to place or remove a note.
FX
Perform. The 27 keys become pads: wake and sleep members, launch sections, hold fills, stutters and echoes, start song mode or autopilot.
SCL
Key and chords. Change key, scale, tempo and swing. Press again for a new chord set.
ARP
Visuals. Four full-screen visualisers.
SAVE
Cartridge. The six-letter password for this sketch, save slots, and nine skins.
REC
While playing, notes you tap on the keys are written into the selected part.

This runs in your browser, so the sounds are stand-ins for the FM-1's own engines and the control layout is approximate. Every screen uses only what the device has: a 240 by 240 screen, 7 knobs, 14 buttons and 27 keys. Keyboard: Space plays, arrow keys turn knobs 1 and 2, Enter repeats the current screen's button.
